How to measure

A quick look at how each measurement is properly taken
Bust — tape kept level, around the fullest point
Waist — at the natural waistline
Shoulder — straight across, unstretched
Reference photos — free-license stock images (Pexels), for illustration only.
Shoulder Straight across the back, from the edge of one shoulder to the other.
Blouse & sleeve length From the shoulder point straight down to where each should end.
Armhole Around the arm where it meets the shoulder seam, not too tight.
Front & back neck depth From the neckline seam straight down to where you want the front/back cut to sit.
Cup size If you know it from a well-fitted bra, use that. Otherwise leave it — Angel will check at your fitting.
