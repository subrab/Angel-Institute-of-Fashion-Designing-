Angel started as a tailoring shop and grew into a fashion design institute without ever losing the hands-on, made-to-order way of working. Every student learns on the same floor where real orders are stitched — and every customer's piece is made by people who teach the craft, not just practise it.

Two course tracks, three durations. Course fees and full curriculum details are shared directly — reach out and we'll help you pick the right fit.

🛡️

Registered business — Angel operates as a registered enterprise under Udyam (Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises, Government of India), confirming it as a legitimate, formally registered institution. Udyam Registration Number: Pending — to be added

Separately, the 6-month course is Angel's own certification program — students who complete it receive an Angel Institute Certification in Fashion Designing / Tailoring, awarded by Angel, not by the Udyam registration itself.

🎓

Trainer, certified at DSOFT — Angel's lead trainer holds a certification in Fashion Designing & Tailoring from DSOFT (Designing School of Fashion Technology), the Chennai institute founded by actress Saranya Ponvannan.

This reflects the trainer's individual training background — Angel Institute is independently run and not affiliated with or endorsed by DSOFT.
